# GreenHorizon — refreshed design direction

## Direction: Illustrated Theme Club

The previous dark archive felt too flat and too close to a static catalog. The new direction is a light editorial marketplace with bold typography, expressive animated SVG illustration, playful color, and a clearer creator upload moment.

- **Palette:** warm paper `#F8F7F2`, ink `#20242B`, signal blue `#4C7CFF`, lime `#B8F24A`, coral `#FF8F72`, mint `#65CFA6`, violet `#B58AFF`.
- **Illustration language:** geometric monitor, orbit rings, sun, waves, stars, and floating UI labels. All are CSS/SVG-native so they stay sharp and animate without video weight.
- **Interaction:** hover lift on cards, breathing orb artwork, orbiting rings, floating hero device, direct drag-and-drop theme-pack picker.
- **Typography:** Space Grotesk for display, DM Sans for readable Bengali UI, DM Mono for small system labels.
- **Brand voice:** warm, direct, creator-friendly. “আপনার pack শেয়ার করুন” replaces the previous abstract archive language.
- **Primary creator action:** upload the actual `.zip`, `.rar`, `.7z`, `.theme`, or `.themepack` file directly from a picker/dropzone; no URL paste required.
