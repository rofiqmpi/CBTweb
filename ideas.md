# ডিজাইন দিক: Neon Quiet / Theme Archive

## সংক্ষিপ্ত ধারণা
GreenHorizon হবে একটি মিনিমাল, ডার্ক থিম গ্যালারি—যেখানে desktop customization-এর জন্য curated wallpaper, color system এবং interface mood এক জায়গায় পাওয়া যাবে। ভিজ্যুয়াল ভাষা হবে শান্ত কিন্তু আলাদা: ink-black surface, warm cream typography, এবং acid-lime signal color।

## Design movement
Editorial digital archive + quiet luxury product gallery.

## Core principles
- প্রথম স্ক্রিনেই “দেখুন → বুঝুন → ডাউনলোড করুন” প্রবাহ পরিষ্কার থাকবে।
- বড় ভিজ্যুয়াল, কম শব্দ, শক্তিশালী typographic hierarchy।
- সবুজ accent শুধু action ও active state-এ; decoration নয়।
- Theme cards যেন পণ্যের মতো লাগে—preview, metadata, creator, download intent স্পষ্ট।
- UI minimal থাকবে, কিন্তু empty বা generic লাগবে না।

## Color philosophy
- Ink #111310: primary canvas, calm and premium.
- Cream #F4F2EB: readable text and soft contrast.
- Acid lime #D7FF4F: brand signal, CTA, active states.
- Moss #829065 / Clay #C8A878: secondary metadata and warm balance.
- Cards use deep olive/charcoal variations with subtle 1px borders.

## Layout paradigm
Desktop-first editorial grid with a wide hero split: statement copy on the left, a stacked visual composition on the right. Browse section uses responsive 3-column cards and a filter rail that collapses naturally on small screens.

## Signature elements
- GreenHorizon wordmark with a simple horizon/sun negative-space symbol.
- Lime underline / pill as a consistent active signal.
- Theme cards with a small “LIVE PREVIEW” label and a floating download affordance.
- Soft grain-free gradients and radial light, avoiding stock imagery and visual noise.

## Interaction philosophy
Immediate, reversible feedback. Search and filters update in place; opening a card gives a focused preview dialog; download uses an explicit browser download link and reports success with a toast. Upload opens a lightweight creator sheet and adds the submitted theme to the local gallery.

## Animation
Short ease-out transitions (160–240ms), hover lift limited to cards, no constant motion. Dialog and toast enter with opacity/translate only.

## Typography system
- Display: Space Grotesk, 700–800, tight tracking for headlines.
- Body: DM Sans, 400–600, generous line height.
- Metadata: DM Mono, uppercase, compact labels.

## Brand essence
Curated calm for a better desktop.

## Brand voice
Short, confident, helpful, slightly editorial. Bengali-first UI copy with occasional familiar product labels such as “LIVE PREVIEW” and “DOWNLOAD”.

## Wordmark / logo
GreenHorizon uses a simple horizon line cut by a rising square sun: a single geometric silhouette that remains legible at favicon size and works in a full-bleed square icon.

## Signature brand color
Acid lime #D7FF4F.
